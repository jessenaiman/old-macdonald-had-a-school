System rules

Keep the craft legible.

1
Texture signals purpose

Kraft holds content. Felt marks an action, category, or special moment.

2
Colour identifies character

Use the canonical signature colour before introducing campaign colours.

3
Stitching is structural

Place stitches at seams, edges, and attachment points—not as scattered decoration.
