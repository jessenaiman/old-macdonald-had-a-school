# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Teachers planning lessons for their classes. They prepare by searching for printouts, videos, songs, curriculum-focused activities, worksheets, blank planning sheets, and related teaching resources.

## Product Purpose

Old MacDonald Had a School helps educators discover, organize, and use evidence-based curriculum lessons and songs. Its purpose is to save teachers time during lesson preparation so they can focus on planning their best lesson.

## Positioning

The product brings evidence-based curriculum lessons and songs together with the practical resources teachers need while planning, including printouts, videos, activities, worksheets, and planning sheets.

## Operating Context

Teachers use the website while preparing classes. Their workflow includes searching for teaching resources, finding curriculum-focused activities, and assembling practical materials for a lesson or class plan.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Support discovery and organization of lessons, songs, and practical classroom resources.
- Preserve educational accuracy as a product requirement.
- Do not invent, infer, or fabricate curriculum standards or evidence.
- The website must not add work to the teacher's planning process; interactions should reduce effort and save time.
- Preserve teacher practicality and the existing brand and content.
- Subject and grade relationships must follow the 16-character concept defined in `content/pages/branding/characters.mdx`.
- The academic subject or grade identity leads; character names and role context do not replace the academic text.

## Brand Commitments

The existing Old MacDonald Had a School brand and content are binding. The 16 canonical characters, their approved subject/grade relationships, identities, colors, roles, artwork, and asset mappings are documented in `content/pages/branding/characters.mdx` and must be preserved.

## Evidence on Hand

- `content/pages/branding/characters.mdx` is the source of truth for the cast identity system and its relationship to subjects and grades.
- The repository contains curriculum content, lesson and song data, worksheets, planning resources, and existing brand assets.
- Future work must not fabricate curriculum standards, evidence, testimonials, or other educational claims.

## Product Principles

- Save teacher time during lesson preparation.
- Make practical classroom resources easy to find and use.
- Treat educational accuracy as non-negotiable.
- Preserve the relationship between subjects, grades, and the canonical character system.
- Keep the experience accessible, teacher-practical, and grounded in existing content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessibility is a product requirement. Future work must preserve accessible interaction and readable presentation while supporting teachers with different needs and classroom contexts.
