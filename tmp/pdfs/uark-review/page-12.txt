There was a Little Bunny
(Tune: Sing a Song of Sixpence)
 There was a little bunny, (Make rabbit ears with hands)
 He lived in a stump. (Draw three-sided box for a stump)
 He was so small and furry (Crouch down on ground)
 He couldn’t even jump. (Make feeble effort at jumping)
 Carrot after carrot (Pretend to chomp on carrots)
 Made him grow so big. (Slowly rise to standing position)
 He hopped around in circles (Hop around in circles)
 And did the bunny jig.
