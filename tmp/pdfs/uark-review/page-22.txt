Going on a Bear Hunt
Let’s go on a bear hunt.
 All right, let’s go. (Tap hands on thighs like walking)
 Oh look, I see a wheat field.
 Can’t go around it,
 Can’t go under it,
 Let’s go through it,
 Al right, let’s go.
 Swish, swish, swish. (Rub hands together like swishing through the wheat)
 Oh look, I see a tree.
 Can’t go over it,
 Can’t go under it,
 Let’s go up it,
 All right, let’s go. ( Pretend to climb tree.  When top is reached, place 
hand on forehead and look around. Climb down)
 Oh, look, I see a swamp.
 Can’t go around it,
 Can’t go under it,
 Let’s swim through it,
 All right let’s go. (Pretend to swim)
 Oh look, I see a bridge
 Can’t go around it,
 Can’t go under it,
 Let’s cross over it,
 All right, let’s go (Make clicking sound with tongue and stamp feet)
 Oh look, I see a cave.
 Can’t go around it,
 Can’t go under it,
 Let’s go in it.
 All right, let’s go.  (Cup hands and make hallow sound when clapping together)
 Oh look, I see something.
 I think - it’s a bear.
 say this with suspense in voice
 IT IS A BEAR!
 Let’s go! ( Repeat everything backward and fast - wipe brow - make a big 
sign of relief)
 WHEW  WE  MADE  IT!
It’s Not That Cold Outside
(Tune: “Mary Had a Little Lamb”)
I don’t want to wear my coat, wear my coat, wear my coat.
I don’t want to wear my coat.
It’s not that cold outside.
I don’t want to wear my gloves, wear my gloves, wear my gloves.
I don’t want to wear my gloves.
It’s not that cold outside.
I don’t want to wear my cap, wear my cap, wear my cap.
I don’t want to wear my cap.
It’s not that cold outside.
SNEEZE!
