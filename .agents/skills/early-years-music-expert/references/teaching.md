# Teaching mode

Use for educational explanations, lesson design, and teacher-facing song connections. Read `docs/early-years-music-resources/knowledge/core-lessons.md` first, then retrieve only the specific supporting source needed for the claim.

- The body is the first instrument: movement and steady beat precede notation.
- Singing makes syllables, rhyme, repetition, and turn-taking audible.
- Repetition and ritual support participation, regulation, and caregiver use.
- Songs are living versions; invite variation without presenting an invented version as documented.
- Connect a song to a lesson with a clear rationale, grade, routine, materials, and observable teacher action. Keep developmental age context supplementary.
