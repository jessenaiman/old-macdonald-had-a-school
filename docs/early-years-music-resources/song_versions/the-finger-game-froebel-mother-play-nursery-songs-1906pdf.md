# The Finger Game

Up and down the fingers go,
Now with speed and now more slow,
A lark's song joyous rings,
When to heaven he spreads his wings,
Gladly we the clavier seek,
When our hearts in song would speak.

Up and down the finger springs,
Still its song the clavier sings.
Now my child thy hand is small,
Fingers weak the tone to call,
Yet it gives a dear delight
When the notes with song unite.

Though so few the notes we hear,
Sweet the cadence is and clear.
