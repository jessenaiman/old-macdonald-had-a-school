# The Counting Lesson

(Right hand.)

Here is the beehive. Where are the bees?
Hidden away where nobody sees.
Soon they come creeping out of the hive -
One! - two! - three! four! five!

(Left hand.)

Once I saw an ant-hill
With no ants about;
So I said, "Dear little ants,
Won't you please come out?"
Then as if the little ants
Had heard my call -
One! two! three! four! five came out!
And that was all!

## Source-verified boundary note

The right-hand beehive and left-hand ant-hill passages are the first and
second verses of this one source item.
