# All The Fish

All the fish are swimming in the water
Swimming in the water
Swimming in the water
All the fish are swimming in the water
All day long.

**Printed song-level actions:**

- Continue with “Great big fish are swimming in the water...” and sing with a low/loud voice.
- Continue with “Tiny little fish are swimming in the water...” and sing with a high/quiet voice.

The two continuation prompts are not complete printed verses.
