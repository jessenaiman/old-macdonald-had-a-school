Five Little Pumpkins
 Five little pumpkins sitting on the gate
 The first one said, “Oh my it’s getting late”
 The second one said, “There are bats in the air”
 The third one said, “Oh, I don’t care”
 The fourth one said, “Let’s run, let’s run”
 The fifth one said, “I’m ready for some fun!”
 The Oooo went the wind (Run fingers)
 and out went the lights (Clap)
 Then those five little pumpkins rolled out of sight. (Roll hands/arms)
