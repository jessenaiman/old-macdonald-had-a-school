- [ ] The page frontmatter preserves `workbook_row`, `channel_order`, canonical title, slug, all DB IDs, album titles, and track numbers.
- [ ] The frontmatter preserves the source workbook path and a provenance note; source-provided fields are not silently rewritten as editorial facts.
- [ ] The age range is present; grade is explicitly `not supplied` or backed by a separate verified curriculum mapping.
- [ ] The owned video asset is attached or its canonical URL/path is resolved; the file opens, is readable, and is playable on the intended classroom device.
- [ ] The audit records whether the video was actually inspected. No page uses language such as “shows” or “visually demonstrates” until that inspection occurs.
- [ ] YouTube and Kathy Reid-Naiman URLs are checked in the publishing environment, with response/link state and the checked date recorded.
- [ ] Kathy Reid-Naiman is credited for the song/recording/lyrics, with album, track, and DB identifiers preserved where supplied.
- [ ] Jesse is credited as owner of the educational video production and visuals; the project-use permission attestation is retained separately from music attribution.
- [ ] The page states the directly observed educational concepts only after media review; workbook-derived candidate concepts remain labelled as pending until then.
- [ ] Before, during, pause, and after prompts are practical for teacher preparation between lessons and do not imply unsupported curriculum outcomes.
- [ ] The printable opportunity has a defined purpose, child-facing activity type, directions, answer/key guidance where appropriate, differentiation, and accessibility/safety review.
- [ ] The chosen `music` or `video` template is marked editorial and has a short rationale tied to the resource’s actual presentation mode.
- [ ] The final Markdown frontmatter agrees with the source queue on title, source row, age, media URL/path, and attribution, and records the editorial template and review state.
- [ ] The page can be previewed, printed cleanly, and saved or opened for classroom use without exposing unresolved local paths as if they were public URLs.
- [ ] A reviewer signs off on content, media, attribution/permissions, links, printable output, responsive layout, and the page’s final `accepted` status.
