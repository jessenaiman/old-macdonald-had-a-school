## Product objective

Build a highly efficient teacher-resource blog in modern Next.js. Figma is the
visual source of truth. Markdown/MDX is the content source of truth. The site is
for teachers preparing between lessons: quickly preview and print resources or
find and save classroom videos. Lessons use two reusable post templates:
music-led and video-led. No SQLite is currently needed.
