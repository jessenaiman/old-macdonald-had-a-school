## Lesson goal

Students recount two or three key ideas from a shared text or media source in a logical order.

## Prepare

- Choose a short read-aloud excerpt or age-appropriate information clip approved by the school.
- Prepare a **first - next - important idea** organiser.
- Display the difference between a key idea and an interesting but smaller detail.

## Teaching sequence

1. Preview the organiser and tell students they will listen for ideas worth carrying forward.
2. Read or view once without stopping.
3. Model adding one key idea and explain why it matters to the whole text or source.
4. Revisit the source in short sections. Partners add two more ideas with pictures, words, or dictated notes.
5. Students recount using their organiser. Invite listeners to name an idea they heard, then revise the organiser if a key idea is missing.

## Print-ready key-idea organiser

| First | Next | Important idea / what I learned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| __________ | __________ | __________ |

## Access and observation

- Provide still images, page markers, audio replay, icons, and adult scribing.
- Let students recount orally, with picture sequence cards, through AAC, or in a home language before supported English sharing.
- Notice whether students select an idea that represents the source rather than merely repeat a favourite detail.
