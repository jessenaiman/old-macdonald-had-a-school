## Lesson goal

Students build on a partner's idea or ask a clarification question connected to what the partner said.

## Prepare

- Display stems: **I want to add ...**, **I agree because ...**, **Can you explain what you mean by ...?**
- Choose a low-stakes prompt linked to current learning.
- Provide a small conversation tracker with two turn boxes.

## Teaching sequence

1. Model a partner exchange in which the second speaker names the first idea before adding or asking.
2. Identify the difference between changing the subject and building on an idea.
3. Give silent think time and a chance to rehearse with a sentence stem.
4. Partners exchange two turns. Students may use a written, visual, AAC, or home-language rehearsal before sharing in English if that supports participation.
5. Debrief with anonymous examples: Which words showed that the second speaker listened?

## Print-ready conversation tracker

| My partner said ... | I added / asked ... |
| --- | --- |
| __________ | __________ |

## Access and observation

- Offer a rehearsal partner, visual stems, wait time, and pass/rejoin choices.
- Do not equate eye contact or quick speech with listening.
- Notice whether the student refers to a prior idea, even with a stem or adult support.
