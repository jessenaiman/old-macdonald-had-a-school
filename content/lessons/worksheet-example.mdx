## Prepare the invitation
- Place one shaker and one drum where children can see and reach them.
- Keep a voice-only choice available; holding an instrument is never required.
- Leave enough floor space for children to sit, stand, or move with the beat.

## Lead the sound exploration
- **Identify:** Name each instrument and listen to its sound.
- **Imitate:** Echo one short sound or beat together.
- **Move:** Invite children to show the sound with their bodies.
- **Join in:** Let each child choose shaker, drum, or voice only.

## Offer flexible ways to join
- Shake, tap, hum, sing, gesture, watch, or listen.
- Repeat the same short pattern so children can enter when they are ready.
- Pair children for turn-taking without requiring a performance.

## Notice the learning
- Chooses an instrument or another way to participate.
- Keeps a beat briefly or responds to a change in sound.
- Shares space or materials with a peer.
