## Curriculum connection
- Workbook topic: Drumming Circle & Body Percussion
- Workbook skill: Children use hand drums, shakers, clapping, and stomping to explore rhythm and dynamics. Develops auditory discrimination and group music-making.

## Participation
- Sequence: Listen → Hum along
- Listening, humming, clapping, or stomping are valid ways to join the group.

## Teacher observation
- Notice attention to the group sound and any vocal, rhythmic, or movement response.
