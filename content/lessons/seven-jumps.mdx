## Curriculum connection
- Workbook topic: Dancing Freeze Game
- Workbook skill: Dance freely to music and freeze when it stops. Builds body control, listening skills, and self-regulation of movement.
- Related workbook topic: Jumping with two feet

## Participation
- Sequence: Move → Freeze
- Children may use a small gesture, seated movement, or whole-body movement.

## Teacher observation
- Notice a response to the stop cue and any return to movement when the music resumes.
