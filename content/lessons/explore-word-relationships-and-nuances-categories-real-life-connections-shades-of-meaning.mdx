## Lesson goal

Students place familiar words in a category and explain one real-life connection or difference.

## Prepare

- Make picture-word cards for classroom objects, actions, and feelings.
- Create three headers: **belongs with**, **could go with**, **does not fit yet**.
- Include two feeling words with different intensity, such as calm and excited.

## Teaching sequence

1. Model sorting three cards and thinking aloud: "These belong together because ..."
2. Ask students to sort in small groups. Allow more than one defensible grouping if students can explain it.
3. Pause for one "does not fit yet" card. Invite a respectful challenge or alternate home-language connection.
4. Compare two related feeling or movement words. Act, draw, or place them on a quiet-to-big line.
5. Close by adding one student-generated category to the class word wall.

## Access and observation

- Use photographs, objects, or multilingual labels beside print words.
- Let students move cards, point, or explain through a partner rather than requiring writing.
- Notice the reason a student gives for a relationship; different valid categories are useful evidence of word knowledge.
