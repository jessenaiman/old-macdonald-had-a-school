## Curriculum connection
- Workbook topic: Circle time routine
- Workbook topic: Turn-taking
- Workbook activity: Sharing Circle – Taking Turns
- Workbook skill: Pass a special stuffed animal around the circle; each child holds it for a moment. Practices patience, waiting, and group participation.

## Participation
- Sequence: Sit together → Listen → Take a turn
- A child may sit with the group and listen before taking a turn.

## Teacher observation
- Notice waiting, attending to a peer, and joining the shared routine.
