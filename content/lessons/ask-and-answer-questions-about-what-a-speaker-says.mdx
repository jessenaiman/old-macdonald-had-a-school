## Lesson goal

Students listen to a classmate or adult speaker, restate one idea, and ask a question that clarifies or extends it.

## Curriculum standards

- Ontario A1; Ontario LA A1
- Ontario Language 2023 B1.1
- US Common Core SL.1.3 (ask and answer questions about what a speaker says to gather additional information)
- US Common Core SL.2.3 (extension standard for students ready to ask questions that clarify)

## Prepare

- Prepare one 30-second message about a classroom routine (how snack works, where the book bin lives) or invite a guest speaker such as the librarian.
- Post sentence stems: **I heard ...** / **Can you tell me more about ...?** / **What does ___ mean?**
- Print or draw the listening card below, one per student.

## Teaching sequence

1. **Name the job (1 minute).** Tell students they will listen for *one* idea — not every word — and then help the speaker with a question.
2. **Deliver the message once.** Wait quietly for thinking time before anyone speaks.
3. **Model it (3 minutes).** Say "I heard that ..." and restate one idea, then ask one question that matches the message.
4. **Partner rounds (12 minutes).** Partners take turns being speaker and listener. The listener restates one idea, then chooses or writes a question stem. Swap roles.
5. **Sort together (7 minutes).** Invite a few questions and sort them as a class into "already answered" and "helps us learn more." Ask what made the helpful ones helpful.

## Access and observation

- Repeat the message, add a photo or gesture, and allow an adult to scribe for students who need it.
- Offer a choice between two question stems before asking students to generate their own.
- Notice whether students connect their question to what was actually said, and whether they wait for the speaker to finish.
- Graduated participation: restating with a gesture or pointing at a picture of the idea counts as a full response.
