## Lesson goal

Students listen to a speaker, restate one idea, and ask or choose a question about what they heard.

## Prepare

- Prepare a 30-second message about a classroom routine or a photo.
- Display sentence starters: **I heard ...**, **Can you tell me more about ...?**, **What does ___ mean?**
- Provide a draw-and-tell response card.

## Teaching sequence

1. Tell students they will listen for one idea, not every word.
2. Deliver the short message once. Wait quietly for thinking time.
3. Model: "I heard that ..." and ask one question that matches the message.
4. Replay or repeat the message. Partners draw/write one idea and choose a question stem.
5. Invite a few questions. Sort them together into "answered already" and "helps us learn more."

## Print-ready listening card

| I heard ... | My question |
| --- | --- |
| __________ | __________ |

## Access and observation

- Repeat the message, add a photo/gesture, and permit an adult to scribe.
- Offer a choice between two question stems before asking students to generate one.
- Notice whether students connect their question to what was actually said.
