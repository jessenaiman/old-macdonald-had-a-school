## Lesson goal

Students ask or answer one question about a shared text or media clip and identify the detail that supports the answer.

## Prepare

- Choose a brief picture book excerpt or short, school-approved media clip.
- Make four question-stem cards: **Who? What? Where? How do you know?**
- Display a two-column chart: **Our question** / **Detail we found**.

## Teaching sequence

1. Preview the question stems and explain that strong answers can point back to the shared text or clip.
2. Read or view once for enjoyment without stopping.
3. Model one question and turn to the relevant page/frame to locate a detail.
4. Invite pairs to choose or generate one question. Reread or replay the relevant section.
5. Share two examples, naming the detail rather than praising only a quick answer.

## Print-ready question card

## Access and observation

- Offer question cards with icons and let students answer by pointing, drawing, acting, signing, or speaking.
- Provide the exact page/frame again for students who need memory support.
- Notice whether a student returns to a shared detail, even with adult prompting.
