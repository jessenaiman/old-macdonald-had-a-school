## Lesson goal

Students use one target word from a shared text or conversation and add a reason using **because**.

## Prepare

- Select one useful word from the current class read-aloud or discussion.
- Create a word card with a child-friendly explanation, picture, and home-language space.
- Display the frame: **I think ___ because ___.**

## Teaching sequence

1. Revisit the target word in its original sentence or conversation.
2. Invite students to offer examples and non-examples. Keep the explanation child-friendly.
3. Model one opinion or observation that uses the word and **because**.
4. Give partners a picture prompt. Each student can speak, sign, point to a prepared reason, or dictate a response.
5. Select two examples to reread, listening for both the word and a reason.

## Print-ready sentence frame

| New word: __________ | I think __________ because __________. |
| --- | --- |
| Picture/translation: __________ | My drawing or words: __________ |

## Access and observation

- Provide a choice board of reasons, visuals, and multilingual word labels.
- Separate the thinking from handwriting; scribe or record oral language where needed.
- Notice whether the student connects the target word to an idea, even if the conjunction is modeled first.
