## Curriculum connection
- Workbook topic: Planting Seeds – Life Cycle Discovery
- Workbook skill: Plant seeds in clear cups. Observe daily growth, water, and measure. Develops scientific inquiry, sequencing, patience, and understanding of living things.

## Participation
- Sequence: Watch → Copy the motion
- Watching is a valid first way to join.

## Teacher observation
- Notice whether the child watches, copies one gentle motion, or anticipates the next part of the sequence.
