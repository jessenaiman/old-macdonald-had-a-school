## Lesson goal

Students use context and word parts to propose a meaning, then reread to confirm or revise it.

## Prepare

- Select two short, age-appropriate sentences with a compound or multiple-meaning word.
- Display the routine: **Read around it - Notice parts - Try a meaning - Check**.
- Prepare word cards with a target word and removable word-part cards.

## Teaching sequence

1. Read the first sentence and model noticing nearby clues before giving a definition.
2. Pull apart a familiar compound word and discuss what its parts suggest.
3. Reread to test whether the proposed meaning fits the sentence.
4. Use a second sentence with the same multiple-meaning word. Compare which meaning fits each context.
5. Students complete one word-solving card with a partner, then explain the clue that mattered most.

## Print-ready word-solving card

| Word | Context clue | Word part clue | My meaning | Check by rereading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| __________ | __________ | __________ | __________ | fits / revise |

## Access and observation

- Read text aloud and provide picture/context strips before asking for print-based analysis.
- Offer movable word-part cards and allow oral explanation or dictation.
- Notice whether students use a clue and revise when needed; a first guess is not a failure.
