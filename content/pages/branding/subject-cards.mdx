## Subject cards reuse the character frame

The homepage subject card is a real use of the same `BrandIdentityCard` structure shown in the cast section. The subject remains the title and destination. The assigned learner supplies the local colour, felt, and unchanged portrait. The curriculum icon stays a separate visual signal.

Use a fastener only when the card sits on a board. It must cross the card edge and the cork surface, as these examples do.
