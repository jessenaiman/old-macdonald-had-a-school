## Character-led subject cards

Use this larger `BrandIdentityCard` variant when a subject genuinely needs a named learner or teacher. The subject remains the title and destination. The assigned character supplies the local colour, felt, and unchanged portrait. The curriculum icon stays a separate visual signal.

Use a fastener only when the card sits on a board. It must cross the card edge and the cork surface, as these examples do.

### Homepage notes and folk-arts links

The homepage uses the reusable `HomeSubjectNote` pattern so six subjects remain visible together without assigning a character to every subject. Each approved blank paper silhouette holds live HTML text, a separate curriculum icon, and an independently positioned fastener. This keeps the notes responsive and makes the attachment logic visible rather than baking a whole card into one image.
