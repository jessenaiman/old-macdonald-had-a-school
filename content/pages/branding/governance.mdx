# Production asset boundary

## Approved production families

- Canonical transparent portraits from `public/staff_and_students/`
- Repeatable authored materials from `public/design-assets/web-material-library-v1/`
- Governed cork from `public/design-assets/cork-board-kit-v1/`
- Separated fasteners from `public/design-assets/classroom-fasteners-*/individual-icons/`
- Grade and subject icons from `public/brand-kit-icon-sheets/individual-icons/`
- Approved blank patches from `public/design-assets/blank-felt-patches-v1/individual-patches/`

## Never use as production components

- Contact sheets, atlases, or mixed asset sheets
- Page composites and page-section crops
- `figma-copy-design/`
- `public/design-concepts/`
- `public/design-explorations-v5/`
- Any rectangle or export marked `DO NOT USE`
- Flat colours, gradients, CSS noise, emoji, or pseudo-elements pretending to be cork, felt, woven cloth, cardboard, stitching, or torn paper
