## Character badge recipe

A character badge has three independent layers: an authored blank patch, the matching canonical portrait, and a real attachment only when the badge is physically attached to another surface. Never recolour the portrait or replace the patch with a CSS circle.

<ol className="list-inside space-y-1 break-words pl-0">
  <li>Choose the character from <code>cast.mdx</code>.</li>
  <li>Use the matching approved blank patch.</li>
  <li>Place the unchanged transparent portrait over it.</li>
  <li>Keep readable text outside the portrait silhouette.</li>
  <li>Add a fastener only where it crosses the badge and its supporting surface.</li>
</ol>
