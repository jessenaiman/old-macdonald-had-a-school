## Character badge recipe

A character badge has three independent layers: a live semantic character surface, the matching transparent portrait, and a real attachment only when the badge is physically attached to another surface. Never recolour the portrait or store another colored-circle image for a theme variation.

<ol className="flex list-inside flex-col gap-1 break-words pl-0">
  <li>Choose the character from <code>cast.mdx</code>.</li>
  <li>Apply the matching <code>cast-*</code> class and <code>character-surface</code>.</li>
  <li>Place the unchanged transparent portrait over it.</li>
  <li>Keep readable text outside the portrait silhouette.</li>
  <li>Add a fastener only where it crosses the badge and its supporting surface.</li>
</ol>
