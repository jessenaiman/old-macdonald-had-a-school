# Cast, teaching roles, and visual ownership

Character colour uses one of six teachable families: **red, yellow, blue, orange, green, or purple**. It identifies a character's portrait panel, note, activity, or teaching context; it is not a page-wide grade theme. Pink is named as a tint of red. Portraits remain canonical and are never recoloured.

## Reusable character-card recipe

Use `CharacterCard` anywhere a character needs a portrait, local colour, role, and safe activity context. The component owns the consistent frame, felt texture, responsive portrait, and hover/focus treatment. This Markdown remains the readable source of the actual content.

### Staff rule

The staff roster is eight. Mr Puddles, Mr Maisy, and Miss Maisy are staff. Mr Maisy and Miss Maisy are also Maisy's parents. Sam's Mom is not canonical unless added here.
