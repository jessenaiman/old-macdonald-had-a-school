## Cast, teaching roles, and visual ownership

Each identity pairs one unchanged [canonical portrait](#assets) with one signature colour from `theme.css` and the exact facts below. Change a character colour only in `theme.css`; do not pass a colour, portrait, texture, or ink value into a component. Curriculum artwork remains independent: choose and include it through the [semantic icon catalogue](#icons).

### Reusable character-card recipe

Use `CharacterCard` where a character needs a portrait, local identity colour, role, and safe activity context. The component resolves the portrait and themed identity from the semantic character key.

```mdx
<CharacterCard character="miss-puddles"
  role="Daycare teacher and swim instructor"
  meta="Daycare · early learning and swimming"
  activities="Circle time, songs, art, movement, sharing, and swimming." />
```
