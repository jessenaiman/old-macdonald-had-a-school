## One icon, three working sizes

Sizing is a component rule, not a second asset catalogue. Find the semantic asset name in [Curriculum signals already available](#icons), then apply the size that matches its production role.

| Role | Class | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large | `icon-large` | Subject introductions, feature cards, and empty states |
| Medium | `icon-medium` | Lesson cards, grade pathways, and curriculum panels |
| Small | `icon-small` with the matching `-flat` asset | Navigation, compact metadata, and filters |
