## Curriculum icons at three working sizes

Use the dimensional felt asset at large and medium sizes. Use the governed compact asset with its required `-flat` suffix at small size. Do not shrink the detailed felt artwork into navigation or metadata.

| Role | Class | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large | `icon-large` | Subject introductions, feature cards, empty states |
| Medium | `icon-medium` | Lesson cards, grade pathways, curriculum panels |
| Small | `icon-small` with `-flat` asset | Navigation, compact metadata, filters |
