## How to use this page

This is an internal design reference. It is not a curriculum feature and it does not control page styling.

Keep grade ownership in the grade routes and templates. Keep approved artwork in `public/`. Use this page to communicate design decisions between people.

## Grade ownership

| Grade | Lead teacher | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daycare | Miss Puddles | Early learning and calm participation |
| Pre-School | Miss Maisy | Practical organization and choice |
| Kindergarten | Mr Rusty | Rhythm, movement, and steady participation |
| Grade 1 | Miss Hayley | Language, reading, writing, communication, and drama |
| Grade 2 | Mr Maisy | Movement, teamwork, health, and safety |

## Staff design reference

### Old MacDonald

- Headmaster and band leader
- Whole-school character
- Colour cue: `#8B5E34`
- Straw hat, white beard, red plaid shirt, and denim overalls

### Miss Puddles

- Daycare teacher
- Colour cue: `#E8A227`
- Pale-yellow duck, blue gingham dress, bandana, whistle, and rolled mat

### Miss Maisy

- Pre-School lead, school secretary, and gardening lead
- Colour cue: `#55705A`
- Black-and-white cow with a floral embroidered apron

### Mr Rusty

- Kindergarten lead, music, and dance teacher
- Colour cue: `#2C6C9B`
- Warm brown horse, dark vest, blue bandana, and fiddle when relevant

### Miss Hayley

- Grade 1 teacher and drama lead
- Colour cue: `#C9527A`
- Cowboy hat, denim overalls, boots, and a friendly face without painted red cheeks

### Mr Maisy

- Grade 2 teacher and physical education lead

### Mr Sam

### Mr Puddles

- Whole-school art and photography teacher

## Design rules

- Do not change approved artwork to solve a layout problem.
- Grade 1 belongs to Miss Hayley.
- Grade 2 belongs to Mr Maisy.
- Tailwind and CSS own layout, spacing, typography, and colour application.
- Do not add runtime cast logic for styling.
- Update this Markdown page when the internal design reference changes.
