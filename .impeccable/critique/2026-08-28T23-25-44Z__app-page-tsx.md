## Overall Impression

The recomposition works: support/artifact hierarchy reads instantly, and the identity wall is now real color instead of transparent ghosts. Biggest remaining opportunity is rhythm: every section header uses the same h2 + hand-aside pairing, and the What's New videos lost their card affordance when demoted to bare artifacts.

## What's Working

1. **Character identity wall** — 16 exact tokens with correct foreground pairing (dark ink on the five light hues, cream on dark), verified computed. This is the product's heart and it finally renders as designed.
2. **Hero composition** — blue-leather support (rgb(30,42,56)), pinned polaroid, vertical ribbon, taped paper lesson list: three material tracks at desktop, clean stack at mobile, h1 at ~14:1 contrast.
3. **Ruled-paper areas** — What's New and the curriculum planner each own one calm `card-paper-ruled` support; no nested cards anywhere.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P2] What's New videos have no interactive affordance** — demoting the video articles to bare artifacts removed their card surface, shadow, and hover lift; the only signal is the thumbnail play badge. Why it matters: the whole section is outbound links; users need to see clickability. Fix: restore a subtle artifact-level affordance (thumbnail ring/lift on group-hover) without reintroducing card-in-card. Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`.
2. **[P2] Section-header rhythm is monotone** — four consecutive h2 + `font-hand` aside pairs at identical size/offset. Why: repeated cadence flattens hierarchy. Fix: vary the aside treatment (e.g. integrate into the ruled area or drop where the heading self-carries). Suggested command: `$impeccable typeset`.
3. **[P3] Old MacDonald card text contrast 4.27:1** — cream #fefce8 on #A66A32 at 14px bold is below the 4.5 body threshold (large-text passes). Locked identity color; matches the known owner decision already recorded in PLAN.md (mid-hue foregrounds). Fix path needs the DESIGN.md amendment the owner approves. Suggested command: defer to owner decision.
4. **[P3] Mobile character wall density** — 2-column × 8 rows pushes content far down on 390px. Constraint (all 16 visible) is intentional; consider `grid-cols-3` at ≥360px with compact cards if the owner wants a shorter wall. Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`.
