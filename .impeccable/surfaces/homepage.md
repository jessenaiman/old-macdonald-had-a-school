# Homepage surface brief

## Scope and visitor mode

- Surface: public homepage for Old MacDonald Had a School.
- Mode: Persuade.
- The visitor must understand the offer, believe it fits their grade-level planning needs, and act through grade-first navigation.

## Audience and job

- Audience: time-pressed teachers and caregivers planning music and early-childhood experiences for children from daycare through Grade 2.
- Job: orient quickly, choose the grade they teach, and reach developmentally appropriate materials without learning a new planning system.
- Emotional journey: welcomed and understood → confident in the curriculum fit → capable of moving straight into useful material.
- Desired impression: warm, capable, and teacher-useful rather than childish, corporate, or decorative.

## Primary action

Choose a grade first—Daycare, Preschool, Kindergarten, Grade 1, or Grade 2—then land in that grade's right mindset and material set. Use the five destinations already governed by shared navigation; do not add a duplicate homepage grade rail or competing CTA.

## Proof and content sequence

1. Curriculum-linked topics show that discovery starts from what the grade is teaching.
2. The 240-song practical songbook shows useful breadth, with songs treated as documented versions rather than bare lyrics.
3. Teaching sheets show classroom readiness through lyrics, documented actions, source provenance, and printable planning support.

Proof should demonstrate real resources and direct routes, not repeat promotional claims. YouTube, Spotify, worksheets, and Ko-fi remain quiet secondary paths.

## Direction and memorable moment

- Direction: the established Living Classroom Working Wall—an open shared-paper canvas with one clear support → artifact → attachment composition, readable paper, approved materials, and exact semantic ownership.
- Memorable moment: the teacher recognizes their grade as the front door and can move from the welcoming classroom scene to the right curriculum material in one confident choice.

## Constraints

- Preserve the grade-first positioning and five distinct grades; Early Years is a Daycare + Preschool grouping, not a sixth grade.
- Do not author lesson curricula or imply generated educational authority.
- Keep academic meaning before character personality; use characters and texture only in their documented roles.
- Keep dense teacher information on readable paper, text live, controls accessible, and primary touch targets at least 44 px.
- Avoid duplicated actions, nested card mazes, loud support asks, and extra functionality that increases planning effort.

## Unresolved decisions

- None for this pass. CTA hierarchy, proof order, established visual world, and homepage constraints are settled by PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md, and this task.
