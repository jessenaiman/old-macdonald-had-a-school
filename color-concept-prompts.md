# Three Ways to Show the Old MacDonald Color Concept

Each prompt below is written for high-end image generation (FLUX, Midjourney v6, DALL-E 3) or as a design brief for a human designer. They assume you have the corrected Register 02 palette applied.

---

## PROMPT 1: The Color System Architecture
**Purpose:** Design-system documentation. Shows the *logic* — eight hue families, teacher/learner pairs, the formula, the harmony. For developers, stakeholders, curriculum leads.

> A clean, spacious design-system reference page for "Old MacDonald's School" character colour palette, printed on warm cream linen-textured paper. Top center: the title "Register 02 — Eight Hue Families, One Formula" in warm charcoal serif. Below, a single elegant formula rendered as a code snippet in a rounded card:
> ```
> --cast-<learner>-color: color-mix(in oklch, var(--cast-<teacher>-color) 65%, white);
> ```
> with a quiet caption: "One shift. Sixteen characters. Perfect harmony."
>
> **Main body:** Eight horizontal rows, each a complete hue family. Each row contains:
> - A large teacher circle (diameter ~120px) showing the teacher's face-patch illustration — felt-appliqué style, stitched white border, the character's species-appropriate colours (pink pig, brown horse, yellow duck, black-white cow, human) on a solid background of the *teacher's anchor hue* (Old MacDonald warm brown #B87A4A, Miss Puddles golden #F6AF32, Mr Rusty trustworthy blue #3589C4, Miss Hayley rose-pink #D95C86, Mr Sam calm teal #2A9A9A, Mr Maisy warm red #C45D62, Mr Puddles soft periwinkle #5367B5, Miss Maisy fresh sage #6B9A7A).
> - An elegant right-pointing arrow (hand-drawn, charcoal) suggesting "morning light."
> - A smaller learner circle (~90px) showing the student's face-patch on the *learner's lighter hue* (same hue family, 65% mix with white): Maisy #E8C8A0, Penny #F9CB7A, Rusty #72AAD2, Whiskers #E695B0, Sam #6CB1B1, Hopper #E66C71, Puddles #8F9CCF, Scout #8DC4A8.
> - Below each pair: the teacher's honorific name in small caps (MR SAM) and the learner's first name in friendly lowercase (Sam), both in the family's anchor hue.
>
> **Bottom section:** A subtle swatch strip showing all sixteen hues in order — teacher anchors above, learner tints below — each labelled with its CSS variable name. Tiny stitch-motif dividers between families.
>
> **Style:** Editorial, calm, authoritative but warm. Cream paper texture visible. Soft drop shadows on circles (felt-thickness). No UI chrome. No gradients. Just colour, character, and system. 4K, print-ready, 300 DPI equivalent.

## PROMPT 2: The Classroom in Colour
**Purpose:** Pedagogical proof. Shows the colours *working* — on worksheets, cubby tags, bulletin borders, reading corners, math manipulatives. For teachers, parents, administrators who need to *see* it in situ.

> A wide, sunlit classroom photograph (35mm film aesthetic, golden hour, shallow depth of field) inside Old MacDonald's School. The space *breathes* the eight hue families.
>
> **Foreground left:** A low cubby wall — sixteen cubbies, each front faced with a felt-appliqué name patch in its character's learner hue. Hopper's cubby: coral-orange felt, white stitching, "Hopper" in cream thread. Penny's: soft gold. Rusty's: pale blue. Whiskers': blush pink. Sam's: gentle teal. Hopper's: warm coral. Puddles': periwinkle. Scout's: fresh sage-green. Each cubby holds a matching-colour backpack (olive for Hopper, teal floral for Whiskers, blue for Puddles, etc.).
>
> **Center:** A round rug in warm cream wool. Four children at a math station with Mr Sam (pink pig puppet, green vest, red bow tie, round glasses). The manipulatives — counting bears, pattern blocks, measuring worms — are *sorted by hue family*: teal bears in a teal bowl, coral bears in a coral bowl, sage bears in a sage bowl. Mr Sam's pointer rests on a chart where the colour formula is written in child-hand lettering: "Teacher colour + morning light = Me colour!"
>
> **Background right:** Bulletin board "Our Farm School Family." Sixteen face-patches pinned in a 2×8 grid — teacher anchors top row, learner tints bottom row — connected by hand-drawn yarn lines in each family's hue. Push-pins are colour-matched. The board's fabric backing is the same warm cream linen.
>
> **Details:** A reading nook with Miss Puddles (yellow duck puppet, gingham dress) — her book bin edged in gold bias tape. An art easel with Mr Puddles (pale yellow duck, checkered cap, blue vest) — paint pots arranged in the eight hues, brushes in matching jars. A music corner with Mr Rusty (brown horse, blue bandana, fiddle) — rhythm sticks wrapped in coloured tape at the ends.
>
> **Lighting:** Late afternoon sun slanting through high windows — *literally* the "morning light" that lightens teacher to learner. Dust motes dancing in the beams. The colours *glow*.
>
> **Style:** Documentary warmth. Not staged. Not sterile. Real felt textures, real stitching, real child hands. Kodak Portra 400 colour science. 8K, cinematic aspect (21:9).

## PROMPT 3: The Character Portrait Gallery
**Purpose:** Brand identity. The "family portrait" — all sixteen characters together, unified by the palette, distinct by design. For the website hero, the curriculum deck cover, the poster on every classroom wall.

> A single composed "class photo" of all sixteen Old MacDonald characters, arranged in two staggered rows of eight — teachers (adults) standing behind, learners (children) seated in front — on the wooden floor of the school's main barn-room. Warm cream shiplap walls behind them. Late afternoon light from high barn windows paints golden rectangles across the floor.
>
> **Each character** is rendered in the established felt-appliqué / soft-3D style (consistent with the face-patch assets): tactile fabric textures, visible stitching at seams, soft diffuse shadows, plush-toy approachability. But here they are *full-body*, *interacting*:
>
> **Back row (teachers, left to right):**
> 1. Old MacDonald — straw hat tilted back, banjo on knee, boots crossed, warm brown anchor hue echoed in his hat band and boot stitching.
> 2. Miss Puddles — yellow duck, gingham dress, polka-dot bandana, silver whistle catching light, golden anchor hue in her dress trim.
> 3. Mr Rusty — brown horse, blue bandana, fiddle under chin mid-note, trustworthy blue in his vest piping.
> 4. Miss Hayley — human, cowboy hat, red plaid, denim overalls, rose-pink anchor in her shirt's finer check and boot stitching.
> 5. Mr Sam — pink pig, round glasses, green quilted vest, red polka-dot bow tie, calm teal anchor in his vest's lining (visible at collar) and ruler edge.
> 6. Mr Maisy — black-white cow, red bandana, plaid shirt, denim overalls, warm red anchor in bandana and shirt (softened to #C45D62 — no fire alarm).
> 7. Mr Puddles — pale yellow duck, checkered cap, blue vest, camera strap, soft periwinkle anchor in cap check and lens rim.
> 8. Miss Maisy — black-white cow, blue dress, floral apron, red scarf, fresh sage anchor in apron embroidery and scarf's finer weave.
>
> **Front row (learners, left to right, each seated before their teacher):**
> 1. Maisy (cow calf) — confident, forehooves on knees, cream forelock, soft brown learner hue (#E8C8A0) in her collar and hoof-stitching.
> 2. Penny (chick) — brave beginner, wings folded, pink bow, soft gold (#F9CB7A) in bow and backpack strap.
> 3. Rusty (foal) — steady, mane brushed, red bandana, pale blue (#72AAD2) in bandana dots and hoof-trim.
> 4. Whiskers (kitten) — curious, head tilted, lavender scarf, blush pink (#E695B0) in scarf and satchel flower.
> 5. Sam (piglet) — thoughtful, glasses slightly large, green bow tie, gentle teal (#6CB1B1) in bow tie and overalls strap.
> 6. Hopper (rabbit) — mid-bounce arrested, orange vest, warm coral (#E66C71) in vest and backpack zipper.
> 7. Puddles (duckling) — enthusiastic, blue cap askew, periwinkle (#8F9CCF) in cap brim and backpack.
> 8. Scout (puppy) — alert, tan explorer hat, fresh sage-green (#8DC4A8) in hat band and neckerchief knot.
>
> **Colour harmony:** Every learner's hue is *visibly* the teacher's hue lifted by morning light — the formula made flesh (and felt). No hue drift. No saturation clashes. The palette *holds*.
>
> **Composition:** Slightly elevated angle (teacher's-eye view). Generous negative space — the children have *room*. The warm cream wall and floor unify. A single continuous hand-stitched dashed line (cream thread) runs along the bottom edge of the frame like a page border.
>
> **Style:** Heirloom portrait. Timeless. The kind of image a family keeps for generations. 8K, 4:5 portrait aspect (perfect for print and web hero). No text. No UI. Just the family.
